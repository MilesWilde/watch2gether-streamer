LEGACY GIPHY COMPATIBILITY

Only the original 36 historical aliases remain, in LEGACY_CHAT_GIFS, for
lookup of previously sent messages. They are excluded from the built-in
picker and its search catalog. No GIPHY artwork is bundled locally.
The proposed 164-entry GIPHY expansion has been removed.

The built-in catalog now consists of 200 locally hosted Animated Noto Emoji
GIFs by Google under CC BY 4.0. Their source URLs, original and served-file
hashes, mechanical resize changes, and verification results are recorded in
GIF-SOURCES.json. Their credits and full license are in gifs/noto-animated/.
