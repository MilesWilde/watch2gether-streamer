These 200 animated 2D GIF files originate from Google's official
fonts.gstatic.com CDN. Per-file changes (including whether a file remains
unmodified) are recorded in /GIF-SOURCES.json, together with source URLs,
retrieval dates, original source hashes, served-file hashes, file sizes,
dimensions, and animation frame counts. The files are hosted locally,
not hotlinked at runtime.

No endorsement by Google is implied. The artwork is provided without

The 36 historical GIPHY IDs remain lookup-only compatibility aliases for
previously sent messages. They are not part of this local Noto catalog,
are not covered by this license, and are not listed in the built-in picker.
