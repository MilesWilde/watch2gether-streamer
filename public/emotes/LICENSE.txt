All included pack assets are redistributed without modifications.
manifest.json records the pack, version, source URL, credited creator and
SHA-256 digest for every artwork file. Existing shortcodes refer to the
OpenMoji versions; twemoji_-prefixed shortcodes select Twemoji artwork.
catalog.json is generated from that manifest for runtime use; it excludes
per-asset source URLs and checksums to keep provenance out of client code.
Additional OpenMoji shortcodes use openmoji_ plus the lowercase Unicode
codepoint (hyphens become underscores). Neocat retains upstream shortcodes.
The OpenMoji expansion covers standard smileys/emotion, animals/nature,
food/drink, travel/places, activities, objects and symbols, without adding
a second full standard style, skin-tone permutations or extra flag sets.
Artist and search metadata comes from OpenMoji's pinned data/openmoji.json:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/14.0.0/data/openmoji.json

The emoji-pack licenses above apply only to their respective pack assets.
GIPHY reactions remain the property of their respective creators and rights
holders. No license to third-party GIF content is granted here. The app
references external media.giphy.com URLs directly; no GIPHY binaries are

animated GIF content, and visual relevance using beginning, middle, and
ending frames. HTTP 404 responses and a placeholder animation served with
HTTP 200 were rejected. External availability may change over time.
